# FlipBook · demo

A showcase of **FlipBook**, a trading journal for people with day jobs: look at a chart, write down what you think happens next, then see what actually happened — one step at a time — and get the call graded.

**Open the demo:** https://m7-sys.github.io/flipbook-demo/

This build runs entirely in the browser: no sign-in, no server, and no market-data connection. Prices are generated sample data, so every feature works anywhere. Anything you journal or track is saved only in your own browser.

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Flipbook** | Backtesting: six charts of the same coin (T−5 … T), spaced by a time gap or by trading session (Asia, London, New York, weekend). Journal a prediction on one chart and grade it against a later one. |
| **Tracking** | Forward tracks: T is captured now and T+1 … T+5 fill in as time passes. |
| **Board** | Accuracy, calibration, expectancy and streaks from graded calls. |
| **Divergent** | Four coins over the same window, marked aligned, loose or divergent. |
| **Live chart** | The chart engine: timeframes, overlays, zoom and pan. |
| **Audio reader** | Reads the price of the coin you're viewing aloud every 1, 3 or 5 minutes (the 🔊 button in the top bar). |
