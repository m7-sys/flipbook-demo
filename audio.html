Feature

🔊 Audio reader

Hear the price without looking at the screen. FlipBook reads the price of the coin you're viewing aloud every 1, 3 or 5 minutes, on the clock as each candle closes, along with how far it moved over that time.

“BERA, 0.2441. Up 0.32 percent over 5 minutes.”

Try it

Coin
Every

Turn your sound on. Prices here are sample data.

  • In the app it's the 🔊 button in the top bar of every page. Switch it on, pick 1, 3 or 5 minutes, and it keeps reading in the background while the page is open. It's live on this page too.
  • It follows the coin you're looking at in the Flipbook, Tracking or Live chart, so switching coins switches what it reads.
  • Readouts land on the clock (14:25, 14:30, 14:35…), at the moment each candle closes.
  • It uses the browser's built-in voice, so there's nothing to install.
