FlipBook

A trading journal for people with day jobs.

Try the features

Flipbook

backtestopen →

Six charts of the same coin, T−5 to T, each a step later in time: a fixed gap (1 hour to 1 week) or one trading session per chart (Asia, London, New York or the weekend).

Try: set Track to Asia, double-click the first chart to journal a prediction, then click the glowing chart it lands on and press Grade this call. Use ← → to step through the charts, and ╱ Line to draw a trendline.

Tracking

forwardopen →

Start a track and FlipBook captures T now, then T+1 … T+5 as time passes, so you can predict the future and check yourself later.

Try: choose Track Demo · 1-min steps and press Start tracking. The next charts fill in a minute apart.

Tracking Test

weekly predictionopen →

Last week and this week side by side (draw trend lines and levels on them) above a blank chart of next week (Monday 06:00 to Monday 06:00, UTC+8). Call the week's state, draw the path you expect and note what you think happens when. It locks when the week starts.

Try: pick a State, choose ▭ Box, ↗ Up or ⋀ Set and drag on the blank chart, then slide the range under it and press ＋ Add note.

Error Log

reviewopen →

Your prediction for a week above what actually happened. Log the real state, mark the stretches where you were off and write down why. The 📅 sidebar opens any past week.

Try: tick Show prediction to lay your drawing over the real candles, then add a correction note.

Board

analyticsopen →

Your graded calls turned into a scorecard: accuracy over time, whether your confidence matches your results, expectancy and streaks.

Try: switch between Command, Focus and Stream layouts.

Divergent

correlationopen →

Four coins over the same window, each marked as moving with, loosely with, or against the main one.

Try: swap a coin with the dropdown above any of the other three charts.

Live chart

chartingopen →

The chart engine on its own: timeframes, higher-timeframe overlays, zoom and pan.

Try: scroll to zoom, drag to move through time and price, and turn on the 1h overlay.

Audio reader

hands-freeopen →

Hear the price without looking: the coin you're viewing is read aloud every 1, 3 or 5 minutes, with how far it moved.

Try: turn your sound on, pick a coin and press Play a readout. In the app it's the 🔊 button in the top bar.
Prices in this demo are generated, not real market data, so every feature works without an exchange connection. Anything you journal or track is saved only in your own browser. Candle colours and trading sessions can be customised with the ⚙ buttons.
